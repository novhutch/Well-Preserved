# Curated Displays

Photographs of Curated Displays for the City of Savannah Office of Municipal Archives and History

**Artifacts:**

[Back to School](Back%20to%20School.jpg) — Display case with a black cloth background and a sign reading "Back to School: Scholastic Highlights from the Municipal Archives." It holds historic photographs of schoolchildren, color postcards of school buildings, a small portrait card, and typed label cards.

[Back to School cont'd](Back%20to%20School%20cont'd.jpg) — Second display case of the Back to School exhibit, with a color photograph and postcards of school buildings, a historic group photograph of students, an orange flyer about a school transfer deadline, a typed letter, label cards, and a QR code.

[Forsyth Park Fountain](Forsyth%20Park%20Fountain.jpg) — Display case with a white sculpted model of the Forsyth Park fountain at the center, surrounded by historic photographs and prints of the fountain, and label cards.

[Mike Williams](Mike%20Williams.jpg) — Display case with a black cloth background showing a large pen-and-ink drawing, two framed portraits in bright pink and purple, a small photograph of a man, a colorful drawing of a building on a tray, a row of twelve small glass bottles filled with mixed materials, and label cards.
