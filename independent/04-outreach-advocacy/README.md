# 04 — Outreach and Advocacy
**Tags:** outreach, civil rights, community archives

Hands-on artifacts for the Outreach and Advocacy competency: curated display photographs, the StoryRoots project, and educational lesson plans for grades 3-5.

## What this shows I can do

- Turn archival holdings into exhibits for a general audience, not only for researchers.
- Take part in programs, like StoryRoots, that invite the public to help build the historical record.
- Explain archival work in everyday language.
- Help make community and civil rights history visible outside the reading room.
- Work in two kinds of outreach: physical displays and an ongoing public history project.

## What this taught me

Outreach taught me that most people meet an archive through what we choose to show them. Building displays, contributing to StoryRoots, and writing lessons for third through fifth graders showed me how to bring Savannah's community and civil rights history to people who may never visit a reading room. It also reminded me why I became an archivist: the community has as much to add to the record as we have to share.

**Origin:** Created during my internship at the Savannah Municipal Archives (City of Savannah Office of Municipal Archives and History).
