# 07 — Records Information Management
**Tags:** records management

Coursework artifact for the Records Information Management competency: A full institutional records management policy written for a fictional repository (the Second World War Archive), covering the records lifecycle from creation through disposition or permanent preservation. 

**Standard/Framework:** ISO 15489 (Records Management); ISO 14721 / OAIS (referenced for digital preservation) 

**Adapted from:** Modeled on real-world institutional RM policy structure, built out for a fictional archive as a coursework assignment.

**What this artifact covers:**
The policy is organized around the full records lifecycle rather than a single procedure, which is what makes it a substantial artifact for this competency.

Purpose and scope — establishes the policy's authority across all formats (paper, photographic, film, audio, digital, artifact) and all individuals who touch Archive records, including staff, volunteers, contractors, and digitization partners.

Key definitions — a controlled glossary (record, archival record, active/inactive record, classification, retention period, appraisal, disposition, accession, chain of custody, personal data, digital preservation, and more) that the rest of the policy depends on.

Roles and responsibilities — defines specific obligations for the Archive Director, Records Manager, and other roles, so accountability for each stage of the lifecycle is assigned rather than assumed.

Retention and disposition — sets out how records are appraised, how long they're retained, and how disposal is carried out and documented by format: physical destruction with a Certificate of Destruction for photographic/film records, NIST SP 800-88r2-compliant secure deletion for digital records, and witnessed destruction for CONFIDENTIAL/SEALED material.

Transfer to permanent preservation — covers what accompanies a transfer (full transfer schedule, preservation-format digital files such as TIFF/PDF-A/WAV, complete metadata) and how it's recorded in the accession register.

Compliance and audit — ties the policy to the Privacy Act, FOIA, Federal Records Act/NARA schedules, Executive Order 13526, and ISO 15489/14721, and lays out a three-tier audit program: annual compliance audits (15% sample), triennial comprehensive reviews with an external specialist, and incident-triggered audits.

Policy review and version control — a biennial full review cycle, a process for interim amendments when legislation or risk changes faster than the review schedule, and permanent retention of superseded versions.
