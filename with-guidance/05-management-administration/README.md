# 05 — Management and Administration
**Tags:** strategic planning, outreach, digitization

Coursework artifact for the Management and Administration competency: a five-year strategic plan written for a hypothetical university library and archives.

**What this shows I can do:**

Draft a multi-year strategic plan for an archives/library institution, translating a mission and vision statement into concrete, actionable goals.

Break broad institutional goals down into specific objectives and tactics, and assign responsibility for each tactic to the appropriate staff role (Director, Digital Archivist, Public Services Archivist).

Think at an institutional/leadership level — balancing student learning, professional innovation, digital infrastructure, and community engagement as simultaneous priorities rather than isolated projects.

Apply a planning framework (mission, vision, goals, objectives, tactics) that carries over directly into real strategic planning work in an archives or library setting.

Anticipate the staffing and resourcing implications of ambitious goals, rather than writing aspirational language with no path to implementation.

**What this taught me:**

The strategic plan taught me that a goal without a person and resources behind it is only a wish. Assigning every task in the plan to a staff member made me ask, honestly, whether a small archives could carry out what I was proposing.

**Origin:** Created for ARST 6620: Managing Archives, Clayton State University. Written for a hypothetical university library and archives.
