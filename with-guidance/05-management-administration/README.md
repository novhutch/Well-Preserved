# 05 — Management and Administration
**Tags:** strategic planning, outreach, digitization

A five-year strategic plan for a hypothetical university library and archives.

## What this shows I can do

- Write a multi-year strategic plan that turns a mission and vision into concrete goals.
- Break each goal into objectives and specific tasks, and assign each task to a staff role (Director, Digital Archivist, Public Services Archivist).
- Plan at the leadership level, balancing student learning, innovation, digital infrastructure, and community engagement at the same time.
- Use the planning framework archives and libraries actually use: mission, vision, goals, objectives, and tactics.
- Think through the staff and resources each goal needs, so the plan can actually be carried out.

## What this taught me

The strategic plan taught me that a goal without a person and resources behind it is only a wish. Assigning every task in the plan to a staff member made me ask, honestly, whether a small archives could carry out what I was proposing.

**Origin:** Created for ARST 6620: Managing Archives, Clayton State University. Written for a hypothetical university library and archives.
